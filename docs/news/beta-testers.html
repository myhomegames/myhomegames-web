Looking for beta testers for MyHomeGames

← Back to home

MyHomeGames is looking for beta testers — people passionate about videogames, retrogaming, and digital collections who want to try a self-hosted catalog built to organize and enjoy your library in one place.

What testers do

With MyHomeGames you can manage retro and modern games, platforms, collections, metadata, and cover art, and launch titles through the available integrations. Testers try the features, report bugs, suggest improvements, and share honest feedback on the experience. You do not need to be a developer — curiosity and a willingness to report what works (and what does not) are enough.

Especially welcome setups

  • PC, Linux, or macOS
  • Emulators and ROM collections
  • Steam, Epic, GOG, or other launchers
  • Samsung TVs with the Tizen app
  • Moonlight Web and Sunshine for game streaming

How to join

If you want to help, leave a comment or send a private message on the Facebook post, and say which device or setup you would like to test on. Thanks to everyone who helps MyHomeGames grow.
